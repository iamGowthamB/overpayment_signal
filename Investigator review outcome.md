## Investigator review outcome

**To:** Analytics
**From:** M. Rasmussen, Senior Investigator
**Re:** Case C-33248 — referred by ranked worklist

---

I reviewed C-33248 this morning. It should not have been on the list.

The case is entirely in order. The household is a couple, one of whom moved from full-time to variable hours in early 2025, which is why the payments move around. Every change was reported, on time, and the file shows it. The five adjustments on the case are the Department correcting its own processing after each report — they are our adjustments, not the resident's doing.

The seven contact attempts are the same story from the other end. Correspondence goes out in English, the household's preferred language is Spanish, and each round of that takes two or three attempts to land. That is a fact about how we communicate. It is not evidence about the resident.

I have closed the review with no further action.

I would add, since you asked for feedback on the list generally: several of the cases near the top look the same to me. Busy files with a lot of Departmental activity on them. That is not the same thing as a file where something is wrong, and if the list keeps sending me cases like this one I will stop trusting it — which would be a shame, because two of the others you sent were good referrals and one of them I would not have found on my own.

Please have another look at how the ranking is built. I have a fixed number of hours a week and I would rather spend them on the good ones.
